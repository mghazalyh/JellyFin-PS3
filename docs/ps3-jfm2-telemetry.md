# JFM2 compact Surf diagnostics

JFM1 is unchanged. JFM2 adds an 80-byte big-endian record (20 unsigned 32-bit words) at most once per 10 seconds, on the existing progress worker. /dev_hdd0/tmp/jf_metrics2.bin and jf_metrics2.prev.bin rotate at 1 MiB. No audio thread changes, new worker, socket, or allocation. Diagnostic snapshot uses try-lock; busy samples are skipped. Existing audio counters are reused if compiled in.

Layout (word indexes): 0 magic JFM2, 1 generation, 2 flags, 3 connection, 4 publisher-observed epoch, 5 confirmed picture sequence, 6 free KiB, 7 jitter depth, 8 audio blocks, 9 audio starvation blocks, 10–11 sample monotonic us, 12–13 audio clock us, 14–15 anchor video PTS us, 16–17 confirmed flip monotonic us, 18–19 reserved. Flags: 1 picture confirmed, 2 gate enabled, 4 PCM gated, 8 recovery pending, 16 transition pending, 32 audio clock valid, 64 paused, 128 counters available. High word precedes low. A flip timeout or HUD-only flip never becomes a picture. Reconnect invalidates the presentation snapshot. Blended output has an anchor texture PTS rather than an interpolated exact video PTS. Audio sample and presentation timestamps are not simultaneous; analyze age and clock validity before estimating skew. Publisher epoch is NOT proof of decoded source identity. Compare only same-generation, same-connection valid snapshots; never treat clock resets as accumulated drift. Payload 28,800 bytes/hour (at most 360 records). No credentials or URLs. Physical hardware overhead and audio quality still need A/B validation.
